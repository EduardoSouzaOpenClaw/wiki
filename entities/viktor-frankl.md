Viktor Emil Frankl (1905–1997) — Austrian psychiatrist and neurologist, founder of logotherapy. Survived four concentration camps during WWII, an experience that led him to develop his theory that the primary human drive is meaning, not pleasure or power.

## Core Idea

> "O sofrimento deixa de ser sofrimento no momento em que encontra um sentido."
(The suffering ceases to be suffering the moment it finds meaning.)

## Key Works
- *Man's Search for Meaning* (1946) — memoir and introduction to logotherapy
- *The Will to Meaning* (1969)
- *The Unconscious God* (1975)

## Core Concepts
- **Logotherapy:** therapy through meaning — humans are motivated by a will to meaning
- **Freedom of will:** between stimulus and response, there is a space for choice
- **Attitudinal values:** when circumstances cannot be changed, we can still choose our attitude
