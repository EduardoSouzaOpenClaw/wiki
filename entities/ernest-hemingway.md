Ernest Miller Hemingway (1899–1961) — award-winning American novelist, short-story writer, and journalist. Known for works including *The Old Man and the Sea*, *A Farewell to Arms*, and *For Whom the Bell Tolls*. His sparse, economical writing style profoundly influenced 20th-century fiction.

## Notable Quote

> "Leva-se dois anos para aprender a falar e 60 para aprender a ficar em silêncio."

(Hemingway on maturity, silence, and wisdom in communication)

## Legacy
- Nobel Prize in Literature, 1954
- Influence on minimalist prose style
- Cultural figure representing stoic masculinity
