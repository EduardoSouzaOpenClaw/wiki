# Classical Liberalism

Political and economic philosophy emphasizing individual liberty, free markets, and limited government intervention. [[adam-smith]] is considered its founding figure.

## Overview

Classical liberalism emerged from the Enlightenment, particularly Scottish Enlightenment, advocating for freedom of thought, expression, and economic activity. It formed the intellectual foundation for modern free-market economics and constitutional government.

## Core Principles

- **Individual liberty:** Freedom of thought, speech, and action
- **Free markets:** Minimal government interference in economic activity
- **Limited government:** Constrained state power, rule of law
- **Property rights:** Protection of private property
- **Free trade:** Cross-border commerce without excessive restrictions

## Key Thinkers

- [[adam-smith]] — founder, *The Wealth of Nations* (1776)
- John Stuart Mill — liberty, utilitarianism
- David Ricardo — comparative advantage, free trade theory
- Montesquieu — separation of powers
