# Free Trade

The policy of unrestricted commerce across national borders without tariffs, quotas, or other restrictions. A core principle of [[classical-liberalism]] and argued by [[adam-smith]] in *The Wealth of Nations*.

## Overview

Free trade theory holds that open markets allow nations to specialize in producing what they do best, leading to lower prices, greater efficiency, and mutual prosperity. Smith argued against the mercantilist system of his time, which used tariffs and restrictions to accumulate gold.

## Key Arguments

- **Comparative advantage:** Countries should specialize in what they produce most efficiently (David Ricardo expanded this)
- **Lower prices:** Competition and specialization reduce costs for consumers
- **Innovation:** Open markets incentivize efficiency and innovation
- **Mutual benefit:** Trade is not zero-sum; both parties benefit
