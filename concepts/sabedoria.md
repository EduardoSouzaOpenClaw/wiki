The ability to exercise sound judgment, especially about life and conduct. Often associated with age and experience, though young people can also exhibit wise behavior. Key dimensions:

- **Emotional regulation:** Knowing when to speak and when to stay silent
- **Judgment:** Discerning what matters vs. what doesn't
- **Long-term thinking:** Prioritizing future good over immediate comfort
- **Intellectual humility:** Recognizing the limits of one's own knowledge

## Key Quotes

> "Leva-se dois anos para aprender a falar e 60 para aprender a ficar em silêncio." — Ernest Hemingway
